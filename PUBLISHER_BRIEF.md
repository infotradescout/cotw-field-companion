# FIELD — publisher evaluation brief

**Status:** independent free preview; no publisher submission, endorsement, transfer or DLC agreement has been made.

## Player problem

Players move among spreadsheets, separate reference sites, screenshots and manual notes. FIELD connects readable references, automatic local recordkeeping and customizable social outputs without editing game saves or automating gameplay.

## Evaluation surfaces

The public browser version demonstrates animal/reserve/rare-fur and equipment references plus a local-photo share studio. The local Windows runtime demonstrates read-only saved harvest, zone, equipment and career tracking. Public web users do not need an account or player save.

## Partnership ambition

The owner is interested in discussing a possible integration, license, acquisition or official DLC arrangement if the product proves useful. This document offers no ownership transfer, pricing commitment or exclusivity. Any arrangement would require separate commercial, technical, security and rights review.

## Evidence to establish before a commercial pitch

- Players can find a correct animal/equipment answer without setup or explanation.
- Automatic supported counters agree with the game, with discrepancies surfaced honestly.
- Zero game-save writes, no private data in public builds, no hidden cloud upload.
- Reliable startup and history persistence across supported accounts and versions.
- Successful user-created card/collage exports and clear self-reported versus save-derived labels.
- Reproducible bug reports, opt-in satisfaction feedback and repeat usage—not invented user counts or testimonials.

## Current commercial boundaries

The preview is free; optional donations are planned but unconfigured. Current source/data licensing is documented in the repository. No game artwork, audio, dialogue or executable is bundled. Source visibility and attribution do not establish blanket commercial redistribution rights. External contributions and any official package need a clear rights chain before distribution terms change.

This brief is available for review, not automatically sent to any publisher or third party.
